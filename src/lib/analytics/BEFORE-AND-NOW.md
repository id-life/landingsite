# Podcast and player events: what the site sent before, and what it sends now

For anyone rebuilding a GA4 report across the day this version reached production. It covers every event the
episode page (`/podcast/<id>`), the podcast list page (`/podcast`) and the music and podcast player sent before.
The events sent now are described in full in [EVENTS.md](./EVENTS.md).

## How the old parameters map

- Every old event put its value in `event_label`. The new events have no `event_label`; each value has its own
  named parameter, listed per row below.
- `podcast_episode` was `<part>_<title>`: the second part of the episode's category, then its title. For example,
  `id_<title>` for an episode of category `podcast_id`, or `lt_<title>` for `podcast_lt`. Now:
  - `episode_id` is the episode's numeric id, the `<id>` of its address `/podcast/<id>`. It is stable when a title
    is edited, and the title is no longer sent.
  - `show` is the whole category: `podcast_` followed by the old `<part>`, so `id` becomes `podcast_id` and `lt`
    becomes `podcast_lt`.
- A title sent as `event_label` (music and episodes in the player) is now the item's numeric id (`track_id`,
  `value` or `episode_id`).
- The platform names change: `xyz` becomes `xiaoyuzhou`, `podcast` (Apple) becomes `apple_podcasts`, and `spotify`
  stays `spotify`.
- Every new event also carries `layout`, `page_load_id` and, in the experience, `section_id` (see EVENTS.md).

## Episode page

| Before                 | When it fired                                   | Its parameters                                                    | Now                          | Parameters now                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `episode_play` (start) | The play button pressed while paused            | `event_label`: `start`; `podcast_episode`                         | `podcast_play` (a key event) | `episode_id`, `show`, `source`: `episode_page`. Sent once per episode per page after 30 s actually played, whatever started it: the button, the progress bar or a timestamp in the show notes. The button alone missed the other two. |
| `episode_play` (pause) | The play button pressed while playing           | `event_label`: `pause`; `podcast_episode`                         | Retired, no successor        | Pauses say little about listening; how far an episode was heard is now `podcast_progress`.                                                                                                                                            |
| (none)                 |                                                 |                                                                   | `podcast_progress`           | `episode_id`, `show`, `source`, `percent`: 25, 50 or 75, each once per episode per page.                                                                                                                                              |
| `episode_end`          | The episode's audio ended                       | `event_label`: `<part>_<title>`                                   | `podcast_complete`           | `episode_id`, `show`, `source`: `episode_page`. Once per episode per page.                                                                                                                                                            |
| `subscribe_view`       | A platform icon clicked                         | `event_label`: `xyz`, `spotify` or `podcast`; `podcast_episode`   | `outbound_click`             | `link_type`: `podcast_platform`; `platform`; `episode_id`; `item_id`: the platform; `placement`: `episode_page`; `link_domain`: the platform's host.                                                                                  |
| `episode_page_view`    | The page opened                                 | `event_label`: `<part>_<title>`                                   | GA4's own `page_view`        | `page_location` ends in `/podcast/<id>`; `<id>` is the `episode_id`.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `timeline_jump`        | A timestamp in the show notes clicked           | `event_label`: the time as written (`mm:ss`); `podcast_episode`   | `timeline_jump` (same name)  | `episode_id`; `position_s`: the time in seconds.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `section_view`         | The page opened, and each time a tab was chosen | `event_label`: `detail_page` or `comment_page`; `podcast_episode` | `tab_select`                 | `tab`: `detail` or `comment`; `item_id`: the `episode_id`. Sent only when the tab actually changes; the opening of the page is GA4's `page_view`.                                                                                     |

## Podcast list page

| Before              | When it fired                       | Its parameters                                                                     | Now                   | Parameters now                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `subscribe_view`    | A platform icon of the show clicked | `event_label`: `xyz`, `spotify` or `podcast`; `podcast_episode`: `<category>_home` | `outbound_click`      | `link_type`: `podcast_platform`; `platform`; `item_id`: the platform; `placement`: `podcast_page`; `link_domain`. No `episode_id`: the link is for the whole show. |
| `podcast_page_view` | The page opened                     | `event_label`: `<category>_page`                                                   | GA4's own `page_view` | `page_location` ends in `/podcast`.                                                                                                                                |

## Music and podcast player

| Before                       | When it fired                                                                 | Its parameters                                                              | Now                                                                  | Parameters now                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `music_player_start` (start) | The play control pressed while paused                                         | `event_label`: `start`                                                      | `music_play`                                                         | `track_id`: the item's id; `trigger`: `user`. Sent when that press actually starts playback.                                                                     |
| `music_player_start` (pause) | The play control pressed while playing                                        | `event_label`: `pause`                                                      | Retired, no successor                                                | Same reason as the episode page's pause.                                                                                                                         |
| `music_auto_play`            | Playback started, by the control, by a first interaction or by the next track | `event_label`: the title; `music_play_type`: `toggle`, `interact` or `auto` | `music_play` for `toggle` only; the others are retired, no successor | It fired for every track the player moved on to by itself, so it counted tracks played, not visitors who chose to listen. A visitor's own start is `music_play`. |
| `music_player_end`           | A track ended                                                                 | `event_label`: the title                                                    | Retired, no successor                                                | The end of a music track is not a business fact. For an episode in the player, see `podcast_complete` below.                                                     |
| `user_interact`              | The first interaction on the page                                             | (none)                                                                      | Retired, no successor                                                | GA4's own engagement already records that a visitor interacted.                                                                                                  |
| `music_switch`               | A playlist row chosen                                                         | `event_label`: the title                                                    | `player_control`                                                     | `control`: `track`; `value`: the item's id.                                                                                                                      |
| `player_menu`                | The music or podcast tab of the playlist chosen                               | `event_label`: `music` or `podcast`                                         | `player_control`                                                     | `control`: `playlist`; `value`: `music` or `podcast`.                                                                                                            |
| `player_type`                | The play mode changed                                                         | `event_label`: `order`, `repeat_one`, `repeat_all` or `shuffle`             | `player_control`                                                     | `control`: `play_mode`; `value`: the same mode names.                                                                                                            |
| `podcast_menu`               | A show chosen in the playlist                                                 | `event_label`: `immortaldragons` or `longtalk`                              | `player_control`                                                     | `control`: `podcast_channel`; `value`: the same names.                                                                                                           |
| `podcast_link_xyz`           | The Xiaoyuzhou icon of an episode row clicked                                 | `event_label`: the episode title                                            | `outbound_click`                                                     | `link_type`: `podcast_platform`; `platform`: `xiaoyuzhou`; `episode_id`; `item_id`: `xiaoyuzhou`; `placement`: `site_player`; `link_domain`.                     |
| `podcast_link_apple`         | The Apple Podcasts icon of an episode row clicked                             | `event_label`: the episode title                                            | `outbound_click`                                                     | As above, with `platform` and `item_id`: `apple_podcasts`.                                                                                                       |
| (none)                       |                                                                               |                                                                             | `podcast_play`, `podcast_progress`, `podcast_complete`               | As on the episode page, with `source`: `site_player`. Episodes played in the player were not counted before.                                                     |
