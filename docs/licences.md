# Licence register: fonts and 3D models

Every third-party font and model the site ships, with what is known about its licence. Rows marked
**owner to confirm** need the licence documents (licensee, permitted domains, pageview tier) from the owner.
Commercial font files must not stay publicly downloadable from this repository; see ENG-10 in the master plan.
